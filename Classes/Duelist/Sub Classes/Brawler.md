You fight with nothing but your body, turning every punch, shove, and grapple into an opportunity to seize control. You disarm opponents with a well-placed strike, drag them where you want them, and use their own allies as obstacles and shields. No weapon is needed when your hands are enough to break their stance, disrupt their magic, and leave them helpless on the ground. Once an enemy gets within your reach, the fight becomes a contest of leverage—and you intend to be the one holding all of it.

### Innate Ability
When you take the attack action while being unarmed you can use your bonus action to take the attack action again. 

### Martial Ability: A Bit To The Left
When dealing damage to a target with a unarmored attack that that is channeling a spell the target needs to make a Intuition saving throw vs 8 + your STR or DEX modifier. On a failure, you can change the target of the spell.   

### Martial Ability: 
When you are attacked, you can use your reaction to try to swap places with a creature within 5 ft. The target must make a Strength saving throw against a DC of 8 + your STR or DEX modifier. On a failure, you and the target swap places, and each attack that targets you this turn targets that creature instead.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Proficiency Modifier per long rest.
