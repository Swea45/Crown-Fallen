You are a fighter, a creator, and an artist. Though you are capable in physical combat, you prefer to fight with your mind and your craft. Music, dance, and painting are beloved by gods and nobles alike, yet art itself is not easily defined. To scholars and the learned, nature, medicine, and the arcane are arts in their own right. To those who must survive in the shadows, stealth and theft are also forms of artistry.

### Innate Ability
You become proficient in two skills and a tool of your choose. You also become Specialized in a skill that you are already proficient in.

### Martial Ability: Mentor
When a friendly creature makes a skill check that you are proficient in they you can change there modifier to what your modifier is in that skill. 
You can do this equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.  

### Martial Ability: Versatile
When you make a skill check or use a tool you are not proficient with, you can add half of your Duelist modifier to the result.
