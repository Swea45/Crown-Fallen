You are a warrior who exists between reality and the impossible, wielding the emptiness beyond the boundaries of the world. You bend space, fracture perception, and create echoes of yourself that move with a will of their own. Your enemies can never be certain where you truly stand, which version of you is real, or where your next strike will come from. You tear open the boundaries between worlds, pulling yourself and your enemies into strange pockets of the Void where reality itself becomes your weapon. Through shifting images, impossible movements, and attacks that echo across space, you turn the battlefield into a place where nothing can be trusted. You are not simply fighting your enemies—you are making them question which reality they are even fighting in.

### Innate Ability
You can create a silent image of yourself as a bonus action, the image can move and perform the actions as you want, taking a bonus action to command the image. The image have to stay within 30ft of you and have be within sight.

Each image have a AC 10 and whenever it is hit it disappears.
### Martial Ability: Between the Horizon
As an action, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet of you. The target must make an Intelligence saving throw against your Subspace save DC, calculated as:

**8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier**

On a failed save, you and the target are pulled into a temporary subspace, leaving your physical bodies behind at your original locations. Both physical bodies become unconscious for the duration.

The subspace is a featureless void containing a circular arena with a 30-foot radius. You and the target exist within this arena and can interact normally with one another.

The subspace lasts for a maximum of 5 minutes. When the effect ends, you and the target return to your physical bodies.

Upon returning, your physical bodies take on the exact physical state you had within the subspace at the moment you left it. Any damage, injuries, conditions, transformations, or other physical changes gained or removed while inside the subspace are reflected on your physical body when you return.

You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, per long rest.
### Martial Ability: Images of the void
You can have a additional image, and images can move within 60ft of you.
