You are a warrior who exists between reality and the impossible, wielding the emptiness beyond the boundaries of the world. You bend space, fracture perception, and create echoes of yourself that move with a will of their own. Your enemies can never be certain where you truly stand, which version of you is real, or where your next strike will come from. You tear open the boundaries between worlds, pulling yourself and your enemies into strange pockets of the Void where reality itself becomes your weapon. Through shifting images, impossible movements, and attacks that echo across space, you turn the battlefield into a place where nothing can be trusted. You are not simply fighting your enemies—you are making them question which reality they are even fighting in.

### Innate Ability
As a bonus action you can create a echo of yourself. The echo can move freely within 30 ft of you while in line of sight. The echos can't attack or interact with the world.

Each echo have a AC 10 and disappears whenever it takes physical damage. If you die or fall unconscious all echos disappears.

### Martial Ability: 
When you are hit by a attack you can use your reaction to swap place with a echo and it takes your place for the attack. 

Whenever you do this or use your reaction as a echo dies you can create a 10ft radius implosion from the echo. Each creature within this area have to make a DEX saving throw vs DC 8 + duelist modifier. On a fail each creature takes damage equal to your duelist modifier and is pushed 5ft towards the center.

### Martial Ability: Echos of the void
You become proficient in deception.
Your echos can speak and carry smaller objects. 
You can have a additional echo, and echos can move within 60ft of you.
