You learned early that magic is slow without steel, and steel is limited without magic. So you mastered both. You reinforce your movements with arcane energy and shape spells to follow the path of your weapon. When distance would deny you, you let magic carry your blade beyond your reach, striking foes as if the steel never left your hand.

### Martial Tree Requirement
You need a spell focus to cast spells.

| 2   | You gain two 2nd level spell slots.         |

### Innate Ability
You have a basic grasp of magic and chose to wielding both magic and steel. See Spells Rules for general rules of spellcasting and your Spellblade spell list.

You gain one 1st level spell slots.

**Cantrips**
You know two cantrips of your choice from your Spellblade spell list. You can change what spells you know whenever you level up.

**Spellblade Spell List**
Spellblades spell list have include spells between level 0 and 5. 

**Spell Slots**
You gain spellslots through the Spellblade tree. To cast a spell of 1st level or higher, you must expend a slot of the spell's level or higher. You regain all spell slots when you finish a long rest.

For example, if you know the 1st-level spell Fog cloud and have a 1st-level and a 2nd-level spell slot available, you can cast Fog cloud using either slot.  

**Spells knowns of 1st Level and Higher**
You know spells equal to your Duelist level, you can only know spells that you have spell slots for.

Whenever you gain a level you can change the spells you know.

**Spellcasting Ability**
You use your Duelist modifier when casting spells. When casting a spell roll your Duelist modifier and use the result as the modifier for the spell. 

Spell Save DC = 8 + Duelist modifier.

Spell attack modifier = Duelist modifier.

**Spellcasting Focus**
As a Spellblade you can use most weapons as a spell focus. 

### Martial Ability: Quick Recovery
You regain your spell slots when you finish a short rest.

### Martial Ability: Flying Sword
You can create a magical projectile from your melee weapon. You can take a action to attack a creature within 60ft with your melee weapon.
