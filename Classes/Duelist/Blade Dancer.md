### Martial Discipline Tree
When picking this martial discipline you add this tree to the trees that you have access to pick skills from when gaining duelist skill points. Some martial discipline trees have requirements for when there skills can be used.

### Martial Tree Requirement
The Blade Dancer trees skills can only be used whiles duel wielding two weapons.

|     | Blade Dancer                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | You may add your duelist modifier when rolling a dexterity saving throw.                                                                            |
| 2   | You can move thru allays and enemy dose not count as difficult terrain.                                                                             |
| 3   | Whenever you take an Attack action you can roll an additional attack.                                                                               |
| 4   | When you take damage you can use your reaction to move 10ft without triggering a opportune attack. You can do this equal to your Proficiency bonus. |
| 5   | Whenever you make a meele attack you can also target another additional target within 5ft with the same attack.                                     |

### Innate skill
When picking Blade dancer you gain the ability to move 5ft after each attack, this dose not provoke attack of opportunities. This dose not count to the movement you can do in a turn. 

*If you have multiple attacks during a action, allows you to move closer to a target or out of danger in between attacks. 
If you roll multiple dice at the same time you still need to be within reach for the other attacks. Exemple: You have 5ft reach with your attack and have three attacks, you can not roll all attacks and then move 15ft away from the target. But moving 10ft around the target wiles keeping 5ft distant and then moving 5ft away is a legal action.

### Martial Ability:  Blade Storm
As a action targeting a single target you can make attacks equal to your duelist modifier result. You can do this once per long rest.

At level 10 and after you can do this twice per long rest.

### Martial Ability: Petal Dance
When picking this you also become proficiency in Performance.

You can use a action to charm each creature within 15ft of you, each creature need to succeed a charisma saving throw or be charmed for a minute. Each charmed creature can repeat the saving throw as a bonus action. 

Your DC is 8 + Proficiency Bonus + your Duelist modifier. Roll this when doing this action, any repeating saving throws are against the original roll and is not rolled each time.
