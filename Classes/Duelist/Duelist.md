## A Duelists Pride

**Duelists** are those who fight not only to excel on the battlefield, but to refine their skills, their art, and themselves. Some focus on mastering a single specialty, while others strive to be the best at everything. The path of a duelist is rarely straight; what one learns depends on the circumstances they face, the people they meet, and, most importantly, the choices of the individual.

Duelists can be found everywhere, yet rarely are any two alike. They often rise to positions of leadership. They are the people that keep fighting, keep pushing, not letting failure stand in there way of improving.

## To Improve

From veterans to beginners, the journey begins with the search for a challenge, for glory or the graduation for there training. But some are forced to begin for survival or revenge. Some have the patient to wait for the opportunity to challenge them self and some will actively search for it even if the law dose not allow it.

## Creating a Duelist

Duelists can come from anywhere. Some seek glory and challenge, while others fight to protect their homeland from bandits and monsters. Were you an orphan who survived by stealing, or were you born into a military family where skill was everything and constant improvement meant survival? Perhaps you are searching for answers or meaning, trying to uncover the truth behind your bloodline, or you are a veteran who has lost their purpose now that a long war has finally ended now that your skills are no longer needed.

Some have walked this path for years, shaped by hard-earned experience, while others are only just beginning or standing at the point where they must leave their old life behind. Some have already found what they were searching for and now journey out one last time. So tell me—where are you in the life of your adventure?

## First Aid
Starting at 2nd level, you can draw on your combat focus to recover from injuries. As a bonus action, you regain hit points equal to your **Duelist modifier + your Duelist level**.

You can use this ability a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.

From level 9th when using First aid you can also heal a target 5ft from you equal to the amount you gained.

## Rapid Response
Starting at 3rd level when rolling for initiative you can add your duelist modifier.

## Martial Discipline ability
At 5th, 11th, and 15th level, choose one ability from one of your Martial Disciplines that you haven’t already selected.

## Command the Moment
At 9th level, you can give each member in your group a additional action. *(Maybe should limit it to not use multiple spells slots in a turn.)*

Once you use this feature, you cant do so again until you finish a short or long rest. Starting at level 18, you can use it twice before a rest but only once on a turn.


## Battle Luck
Starting at 10th level, once per turn during combat you can reroll a dice before knowing if the outcome succeeds or not. This can be used to reroll attack, hit, saving throws or any other rolls done during combat. 

## Intimidating Aura
From level 14 you can take a action to intimidate any opponents 30ft from you. Each target needs to roll a DC13 charisma check, if failed the target becomes frightened for 1 min.  You can do this equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.

During combat each creature can re roll the check at the end of there turn. 
## Crownbreaker
At 20th level, when rolling for initiative you can roll a second time to have a second turn during combat, only you can be a member of this group. You can only use this once per long rest.



## Spacial Duel (Sub class ability?)
Beginning at 6th level you can use your bonus action to force one creature 5 feet from you of the same size as you or one size larger to a separate dimension until the end of your next turn. When you return you both enter an unoccupied space close to where you disappeared.

The dimension is a 30 feet radius arena, when you and the creature are in this separate space no other creature can interact with you or the creature until you return. You can use this ability equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.
