# Basic Features
Basic features are include skills, proficiencies and character modifications. When creating a character you can pick an amount of basic features equal to your class.

Some Basic Features needs a requirement before you have the option to pick them. If a Basic Feature have the Repeat you can pick this multiple times.


--- 
##### Runeflesh I
Your hit dice become a d8. You can re-roll your health when picking this feature.  

---
##### Runeflesh II
*Requirement: Runeflesh I*
Your hit dice become a d10. You can re-roll your health when picking this feature.  

---
##### Runeflesh III
*Requirement: Runeflesh II*
Your hit dice become a d12. You can re-roll your health when picking this feature.  

---
##### Vitality I
You gain a additional hp equal to your level.

---
##### Vitality II
*Requirement: Vitality I*
You gain a additional hp equal to twice your level.

---
##### Vitality III
*Requirement: Vitality I*
You gain a additional hp equal to twice your level.

---
##### Skilled
*Repeat*
You can pick 2 skills to become proficient in them. 

--- 
##### Armored I
You become proficient with Medium armor and shields.

--- 
##### Armored II
*Requirement: Armored I*
You become proficient with Heavy armor.

--- 
##### Magical Novice
You learn a cantrip and can cast it using your intelligent modifier for spellcasting. If you already have spellcasting ability you can use that modifier for the cantrip.

---
##### Weapon Specialist
*Repeat*
You become proficient with a advanced weapon.

---
##### Marksman
When attacking a enemy with a ranged weapon, gain +2 on hitting.

---
##### Nimble
You gain a additional reaction that you can use between turns.

---
##### Quick
Your speed increases by 5 ft.
