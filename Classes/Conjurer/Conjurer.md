## The Price for Perfection

Conjurers are researchers, students and fanatics. They spend their time practicing and adjusting to perfect their spells, they aren't interested in learning about different kinds of magic but the pinnacle of their chosen craft. They take inspiration from all corners of the multiverse, from the fay and demonic to artifacts and the people walking around them. Conjurers are magic casters specialized in one type of magic, but this takes time and practice.

You can find conjurers anywhere, most of them are normal citizens spending their time searching for information about their subject and become quite knowledgeable about other subjects in the meantime, but if you reveal information they might follow you, bribe you or hunt you to get it from you. 

## Your Obsession

Conjurers have found their thing, their calling, their meaning with life. Conjurers only have one goal and that is to find out everything about their calling no matter what it could be. They are not inherently evil, but the lengths they go to gather knowledge can often be morally questionable.

Their methods vary as much as their backgrounds. Some are born into wealth and use their connections and resources to acquire knowledge and rare materials. Others withdraw from society entirely, dedicating every waking moment to their studies. And there are those so consumed by their obsession that they take to adventuring, willing to steal, kill, and sacrifice, all in the name of research.


## Creating A Conjurer

When creating a conjurer, you need a backstory that would make your character come in contact with your obsession and what he is searching for. Perhaps your character was born to nobles and got an education in battlefield maneuvering and controlling it, maybe he has found the flaws in your humanoid body or forced into it to take revenge and curse someone's existence.

Is this your first action to search for more information or have you driven your other options to the ground, are your funds at an end and you can no longer buy your way to the information or have you have your own research started to only go in circles. Why is adventuring your next step on your journey?

# Class Features
As a conjurer, you gain the following class features.

### Basic Features: 1
### Hit Points
Hit points are calculated using the your hit dice. At first level you get the max of your hit dice and after that you can roll your dice and use the result or use the average of the dice. You have an 
amount of hit dice equal to your class level.

### Proficiencies
**Saving Throws:** Strength, Intelligence

**Skills:** Choose two skills from Arcane, Nature, Investigation, History, Persuasion, Medicine, Deception, Sleight of Hand
### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
- (a) a light crossbow and 20 bolts or (b) any simple weapon
- (a) a Dungeoneer’s pack or (b) an Scholar’s Pack
- Leather armor, any simple weapon, and a arcane focus

## Spellcasting
You have learned your way to use magic and it has become an obsession. Slowly and meticulously you perfect your spells. See Spells Rules for general rules of spellcasting and your Obsessions spell listing for spells listed.

**Obsession Spell List**
Before choosing your spells you need to chose your obsession, each obsession have different spell lists that you follow to know which spells you know

**Cantrips**
At 1st level, you know two cantrips of your choice from your Obsession spell list. You learn additional cantrips of your choice at higher levels, as shown in the Cantrips Known column of the Conjurer table

**Spell Slots**
The Conjurer table shows how many spell slots you have to cast your conjurer spells of 1st level and higher. To cast one of these conjurer spells, you must expend a slot of the spell’s level or higher. You regain all spell slots when you finish a long rest.

For example, if you know the 1st-level spell Fog cloud and have a 1st-level and a 2nd-level spell slot available, you can cast Fog cloud using either slot.  

**Spells Known of 1st Level and Higher**
You know two spells of each level that have access to from your Obsession spell list.

For instance, when you reach 3rd level you know two 1st level spells and two 2nd level spells. When you reach 5th level you learn two 3rd level spells and so on at higher levels.

Additionally, when you gain a level in this class, you can choose spells that you already know and replace it with another spell of the same level from your Obsession spell list.    

**Spellcasting Ability** (Look over use a different system, instead of 5e)

Intelligence is your spellcasting ability for your Conjurer spells, since you seek spells through careful research and studying. You use your Intelligence whenever a spell refers to your spellcasting ability. In addition, you use your Intelligence modifier when setting the saving throw DC for a Conjurer spell you cast and when making an attack roll with one. 

Spell Save DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier.  

Spell attack modifier = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

**Spellcasting Focus** 
As a conjurer you need a spell focus to cast most of their spells, as the focus is specific to your specialty it can be hard for a conjurer to replace it.

## Multitasking
You are adept at maintaining focus on multiple spells at once. You can have a number of concentration spells equal to your proficiency modifier + 1 active simultaneously.

When you fail a concentration saving throw, all currently active concentration spells are lost.

If you cast a spell that does not require concentration, you lose concentration on all active spells. (Maybe)

At 9th level, failing a concentration save only causes you to lose half of your active concentration spells, rounded down.

## Ability Score Improvement
(How Ability Scores work might change)
When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or you can increase two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal you can’t increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. 

Using the optional feats rule, you can forgo taking this feature to take a feat of your choice instead.
## Battle Focus
Beginning at 5th level, you can use an action to enter a **Focused State**.

While in this state, you can make two attacks or spell attacks using our action. The Focused State lasts for 1 minute and requires concentration to maintain.

You can enter this state a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.

## Magic Aura
Starting at 10th level, you can spend a minute to create an invisible shield around you. The next time you would take damage you take no damage instead and the shield is destroyed. The shield does not activate on psychic damage or damage from falling. Having the shield active takes concentration

## Perfection
Beginning at 14th level, with a lot of practice you can cast your spells perfectly if you concentrate. Next time a spell you cast would deal damage you can change all of the damage dice to its highest value. This works for any damage you would make that counts as magical. You do this once per long rest, starting at the 18th level you can do this twice per long rest.

Field Marshals manipulate magic with precision and strategy. They can shift the flow of spells, predict enemy movements, and adapt their magic to any situation. At the pinnacle of their power, they can expand their spell repertoire, making them unparalleled tacticians on any battlefield.
