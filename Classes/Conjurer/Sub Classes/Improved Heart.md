You have seen the limits of your mortal body and sought to surpass them. By turning your heart into a conduit for magic, you have fused flesh and spell craft, allowing your body to support and amplify your magic as long as it beats. Every part of you is now a tool, weapon, and shield, capable of reshaping itself to meet any challenge.

## Fast Adaptation  
Begging of 1st level you can cast _Alter Self_ a number of times equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest, without expending a spell slot.

## Resilient Thought  
Begging of 1st level you have advantage on concentration saving throws.

## Body Modification  
At Level 3 during a long rest you can change your form to one of the following:

- **Body of the Slime:**
	Your body becomes soft and gives in without any resistance. Non-magical bludgeoning damage is reduced by 3, at level 11 the damage is reduced by 6.
- **Body of the Golem:**
    Your body becomes thick and rocky, you resist sharp objects. Non-magical slashing damage is reduced by 3, at level 11 the damage is reduced by 6. 
- **Body of the Zombie:**
    Your body isn't bothered by small holes in it. It quickly stops fluids and organs to become destroyed and slowly regenerate the rest. Non-magical piercing damage is reduced by 3, at level 11 the damage is reduced by 6.

## Imbued with Magic
At 6th level your body has started to remember and keeps the magic inside of it even after your spells have ended. Your strikes made with your body count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistances and immunities to non magical attacks, your Body Modification now also works against magical damage.

## Will of Change
Starting at 11th level you have learned how to change your body to fit the situation. You can harden, soften, stretch, compress and even grow extra limbs. You can take a action to transform to one of the following choices:

- You can grow a pair of wings that allows you to have a flying speed equal to half of your movement speed.

- You can make yourself a pair of gills as well as changing your eyes to adapt to the water, you can no longer breathe air.
 
- You can reshape your arm to become a weapon, either sharp like a blade or heavy like a club:
	- Your bones in your arm hardens and becomes a sharp blade dealing 1d8 + str/dex slashing damage with a 5ft range.
	- Your hand and forearm becomes a massive club dealing 1d12 + str bludgeoning damage with a 5ft range.
	- Your arm stretches and becomes sharp like a spear dealing 1d6 + dex piercing damage with a 10ft range.
	- Like a squid your arm becomes a tentacle dealing 1d4 + str/dex bludgeoning damage with a 5ft range, you have advantage to grapple.

- Shape your fingers and hands to small tools like, hammers, lockpicks or mirrors.

Each modification needs concentration. Modifications are lost if you fail a concentration save.

## Strengthened body (Needs to change if constitution is removed)

At 15th level you gain proficiency in constitution saving throws and your hit dice become a d10

## Eternity
Beginning at 20th level your body has stopped aging and also found how to negate most damage, reduce all damage by 6. Your Body Modification additionally reduces its damage type on top of this ability.
