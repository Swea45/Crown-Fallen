Field Marshals manipulate magic with precision and strategy. They can shift the flow of spells, predict enemy movements, and adapt their magic to any situation. At the pinnacle of their power, they can expand their spell repertoire, making them unparalleled tacticians on any battlefield.

## Shifting Flow
Starting at 1st level you can use your bonus action to move a concentration spell with an area of effect 10 feet in any direction. 

## Remember
Starting at 3rd level, when you lose concentration on a spell, you can use your reaction to recall it and cast it again using a spell slot one level lower than the original. This ability cannot be used with 1st-level spells.

You can use this ability once per long rest. At 11th level, you can use it twice per long rest.

## Magic Sense
Starting at 6th level you can sense characters that have magic within them, using a bonus action you can detect witch characters within 60 feet around you that can cast spells.

### Predictable Move
At 11th level you have enough magic and understanding of the battlefield to predict an attack and dodge using a small teleportation. When you are attacked by a creature that you are aware of,  you can use your reaction to teleport yourself 10 feet in any direction that you can see, you take no damage, use this ability before you know if the attack hits. You can use this equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.

## Perfect Fit
You can change the shape of your spells to fit your needs. Beginning at 15th level you can change the area your spell effects, if a spell you have cast have an area of effect you can change its shape to:
- a 60 feet long and 30 feet wide cone, 
    
- a 30 feet cube.
    
- a 30 feet radius sphere.
    
- a 15 feet radius 120 feet high cylinder.
    
You can use this equal to your proficiency modifier per long rest.

## The Next Step
At 20th level you have practiced over and over and now you have perfected the spells you know. You can choose two additional spells that you know of each spell level.
