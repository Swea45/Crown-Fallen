Those who walk this path refuse to die before their work is done. Driven by obsession and spite, they carry curses like scars and carve their will into creatures, objects, and even souls. Their magic is fueled by sacrifice and unfinished purpose, trading pieces of themselves for power they know will one day demand repayment.

## Unfulfilled Purpose
Starting at 1st level, when you drop to 0hp you regen all hit dice you have left and wake up with the Unfulfilled Curse on you.

When you have the Unfulfilled curse on you, you can’t cast any spells nor gain any hit points by taking short or long rests. If your hp were to drop to 0 while under the curse you are dead and get no death saving throws  This curse can be removed by the Remove Curse spell or by killing a humanoid creature.

## Blinded Spite
Starting at 3rd level you can use your bonus action to cast a spell that targets a single enemy that you have already targeted this turn. You can use this ability once every long rest, at level 11 you can do this twice per long rest.

## Cursed Belongings
Through trial and practice, you have learned to place curses not only on creatures, but also on objects. Long exposure to your own magic has made your body resistant to its lesser effects, allowing you to carry up to three **cursed objects** at a time without suffering their consequences.

Starting at 6th level, you can spend time during a long rest to imbue an object with a **minor curse**, choosing one of the following effects:
- You can sense the general direction of the object, though not its exact location. If the object moves to another plane, the connection is lost.

- A creature holding the object loses 1 AC, this curse doesn't stack.

- When the object is struck, it produces a loud sound you have previously recorded.
   
- A creature holding the object has its movement speed reduced by 5 ft. This effect does not stack.

- Other creatures cannot hear a person holding the object. The holder can still hear themselves and cast spells normally.

If you were to carry more than three cursed objects at the same time you gain all of the effects from the cursed items you are carrying.

## Halting thought
Starting at 11th level, you can use a bonus action to impose your thoughts onto another creature, briefly disrupting its focus. One creature of your choice must make an **Intelligence saving throw**. On a failure, you choose one of the following effects, which lasts until the end of the creature’s next turn:
- The creature loses either its **movement** or its **action**.
- The creature has **disadvantage on its next saving throw**.
You can use this ability a number of times equal to your **proficiency modifier** per long rest.

## Soulbound
Beginning at 15th level, you have uncovered a curse capable of binding two souls together, forcing them to share joy, fear, pain, and relief alike.

As an action, you can attempt to bind two creatures you can see within range. Each creature must make a **Charisma saving throw**; a creature can choose to fail this save. If at least one of the creatures fails, the two become **soulbound**.

While soul bound, the creatures share the following effects:

- Whenever one creature takes damage, the other takes the same amount of damage. This damage ignores resistances and immunities.
- Whenever one creature regains hit points, the other regains the same number of hit points (up to its maximum hit points).
- If one creature becomes poisoned, blinded, or paralyzed, the other gains the same condition.
- If one of them gains a Condition the other also gains it.

The bond ends if either creature dies or becomes unconscious.

This ability requires **concentration**, expends a **3rd-level spell slot**, and you can have only one pair of creatures soulbound at a time.

## Cursed Power
At the end of your journey you have found a way to sacrifice your soul to gain power but with a cost, and you hope the “cost” will not come for you later.

Starting at 20th level you can spend an action to regain a spell slot between 1-5 you can not have more spells than you have spell slots, doing this makes you gain a minor curse determined by the DM, this should be similar to a minor detrimental effect or of that power. You can use this ability three times a day.

You can also spend a minute to regain a spell slot between 6-9 you can not have more spells than you have spell slots, doing this makes you gain a major curse determined by the DM, this should be similar to a major detrimental effect or of that power. You can use this ability three times a day.
