Combat is divided into **groups**. At the start of combat, each group rolls [[#Initiative]]. Groups take turns in descending Initiative order, with the highest Initiative acting first.

During a group’s turn, each member of that group may take the following on their turn:
- [[#Action]]
- [[#Bonus Action]]
- [[#Move]]
The order in which group members act does not matter. Members may take their actions in any sequence: one member may complete all of their actions before another acts, or the group may interleave actions, with different members acting one at a time.

Each creature also has one Reaction, which can be used when its triggering condition is met. A creature can use only one Reaction per round. Reactions refresh at the beginning of the creature’s group’s turn.


## Initiative
When rolling Initiative each member in a group rolls a d20 and add dexterity modifier to it. Add them together and divide by the amount of members in the group.

If a group have a surprise advantage they gain a +5 to the initiative.

## Move
A character may move up to their movement speed on their turn. If the character has additional movement types, such as a climbing or flying speed, they may use those as part of the same move.

When using multiple movement types, the total distance moved cannot exceed the highest speed used, and no individual movement type can exceed its own speed.

### *Example:*
*If a character has a movement speed of **40 ft** and a climbing speed of **20 ft**, they may:*
- *Move **20 ft** on the ground and climb **20 ft** (total 40 ft).*

*They may not:*
- *Move **30 ft** on the ground and climb **20 ft** (total 50 ft, exceeds the maximum allowed distance).*
- *Move **10 ft** on the ground and climb **30 ft** (exceeds the climbing speed).*
