## Basic Ability Modifiers
Each player character and creature has five basic abilities, each with a value ranging from **1 to 20**.
* Strength - Str
* Dexterity - Dex
* Intelligence
* Intuition
* Charisma - Cha

To calculate an ability’s modifier, use the following formula:
(Ability Value − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down.

## Health and Death
#### Player Health
Each player character starts with a D6 as there hit dice, witch can be upgraded using [[Features#Basic Features|Basic Features]]. 
* For the first level you get the max amount of your hit dice 
* All other levels you ether roll or use the average of your hit dice.

Players can still take all actions and moment even if health they have 0 or less hit points. Health needs to be tracked while negative. 
#### Death Throws
When a player character hits or falls bellow 0 hit points they gain the [[#At Death’s Door]] and [[#Unstable]] status, unless they already have it. 

While a creature with the "[[#At Death’s Door]]" status would take damage while bellow 0 hit points the player needs to roll a death throw, if the attack would take the target from a positive amount of heath to a negative the player rolls with advantage. 

To make a Death Throw, roll a D20 vs your [[#At Death’s Door]] DC, on a fail the character die.
#### At Death’s Door
When a creature gains the At Death’s Door condition, its Death Throw DC is set to 5.  
This DC remains in effect until the condition is removed.

The **At Death’s Door** condition is removed by:
- Finishing a long rest
- A spell or ability that removes the condition
#### Unstable
While a creature is **Unstable**, its At Death’s Door DC increases by 5 at the end of each of its turns.

A creature gains the **Unstable** condition when:
- It falls below 0 hit points
- It takes damage while below 0 hit points

The **Unstable** condition is removed by:
- Being restored to above 0 hit points 
- A spell or ability that removes the condition
- Succeeding on a DC 15 Medicine check
