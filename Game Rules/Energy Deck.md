# Card-Based Task System  
  
A deck of cards determines how well a character performs a task. The starting deck contains 20 cards numbered 1 to 20.  
  
When drawing a card, the revealed value indicates the level of success. After resolving the action, the card is moved to the discard pile.  
  
A player can shuffle the discard pile back into the deck whenever they want, or when the deck is empty. When shuffling, the player gains 1 point of Exhaustion.  
  
The deck is fully shuffled and reset during a long rest, and all Exhaustion is removed.
